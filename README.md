# 📊 Bellabeat Data Analysis Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview
This case study is part of the **Google Data Analytics Capstone Project**. The objective is to analyze public FitBit tracker data to identify consumer usage trends and provide data-backed marketing strategies for **Bellabeat**.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Methodology
* **Tool Used:** Google Sheets / MS Excel
* **Techniques:** Data Cleaning, Date Parsing (`WEEKDAY`), Pivot Tables, Aggregations, and Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule) Analysis.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings
1. **Daily Utility vs. Casual Usage:** Device activity is consistent across weekdays, proving smartwatches are viewed as essential daily health utilities rather than short-term gadgets.
2. **The 37-40% Pareto Split:** ~37% of users consistently cross the high-burn benchmark (>2288 calories), while the remaining ~63% stay in low-to-moderate activity ranges.
3. **Outlier Identification:** Identified top 3 peak performers who burn calories significantly above the population baseline.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations (Act Phase)
1. **Incentive-Based Marketing (Top Performers):** Reward top 3 monthly performers with branded Bellabeat amenities (apparel, smart bottles) and digital badges to drive brand loyalty and word-of-mouth marketing.
2. **Behavioral Nudges (Lower Tier):** Implement automated micro-goal push notifications for the 60% casual user group to sustain habit formation and prevent device drop-off.
